<div style="text-align: justify;">
<p>
I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering. My research focuses on making computational solid mechanics more autonomous, reliable, and scalable. My group works at the intersection of computational mechanics, scientific machine learning, and uncertainty quantification, with a particular interest in AI/ML methods that are interpretable and firmly grounded in classical numerical analysis and mechanics.
</p>
<p>
Prior to joining the faculty, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Hopkins Extreme Materials Institute at Johns Hopkins University, following the completion of my Ph.D. in Engineering Mechanics at Columbia University in 2023.
</p>
