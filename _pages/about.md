<div style="text-align: justify;">
<p>
Since September 2025, I have been a tenure-track <a href="https://www.mccormick.northwestern.edu/research-faculty/directory/profiles/bahmani-bahador.html">Assistant Professor of Mechanical Engineering at Northwestern University</a>. My research focuses on making computational solid mechanics autonomous, reliable, and scalable. My group works at the intersection of computational mechanics, scientific machine learning, and uncertainty quantification, with a particular interest in physics-constrained AI/ML methods that are interpretable and firmly grounded in the principles of numerical analysis and mechanics.
</p>
<p>
Prior to joining Northwestern, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Hopkins Extreme Materials Institute at Johns Hopkins University, following the completion of my Ph.D. in Engineering Mechanics at Columbia University in 2024.
</p>
