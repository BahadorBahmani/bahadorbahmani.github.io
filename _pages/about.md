<div style="text-align: justify;">
I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering. My research lies at the intersection of <u>computational solid mechanics</u> and <u>machine learning</u>, with a focus on making computational solid mechanics more <u>autonomous</u> and <u>scalable</u> by developing <u>interpretable</u> algorithms grounded in classical numerical analysis and mechanics. Prior to this, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Hopkins Extreme Materials Institute at Johns Hopkins University, following the completion of my Ph.D. in Engineering Mechanics at Columbia University in 2023.
